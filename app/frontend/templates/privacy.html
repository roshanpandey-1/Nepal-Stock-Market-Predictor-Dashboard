Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

NEPSE Intelligence is a personal, educational project. This page explains what it stores about you and why. You can use the dashboard, stock pages and backtest without an account; nothing about you is stored then.

What we store if you create an account

  • Your email address and, if you give it, your name.
  • A securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself). If you sign in with Google, we store Google's account ID for you instead, along with the name and email Google shares.
  • The stocks on your watchlist, the holdings you enter in your portfolio, and your recent stock searches (used to show your search history and to suggest stocks from sectors you follow).

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares only your name, email address and a confirmation that the email is verified. We do not get access to your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Cookies and browser storage

We use one session cookie to keep you logged in, and your browser remembers your light or dark theme choice. There is no advertising or third-party tracking.

Who else handles your data

The site runs on Render and stores accounts in a hosted PostgreSQL database. Password-reset emails are sent through Brevo, which receives your email address only to deliver that message. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page. This permanently removes your account, watchlist, portfolio and search history.
