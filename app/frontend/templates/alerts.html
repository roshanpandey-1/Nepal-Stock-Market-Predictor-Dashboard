How alerts work. They use end-of-day prices, so a price that touches your level during the day but closes back below it will not trigger anything. Each condition is checked once a day; you are emailed when it turns from false to true (not again every day it stays true), and it is re-armed once the condition stops being true. A stock that did not trade that day is skipped. Headline alerts match a company by name, so some items may be missed. Emails go only to your account address. Delete an alert to stop its emails. Alerts are a reminder to look at a stock, not advice to buy or sell.
